WebGL Virtual Classroom

A small 3D classroom in the browser, built with three.js, that you can walk through on a desktop or in VR.

three.jsWebGLWebXRJavaScriptglTF
GitHub Live site · coming soon

Overview

An early coursework project from my MSc. A glTF classroom scene with animated cat models, a video screen and positional audio, which you can explore with orbit controls or in a VR headset through WebXR. It came with a written design document.

Key features

Explore on a desktop or in VR

Animated models play through AnimationMixer, and a video plays on an in-scene screen as a VideoTexture. dat.GUI panels control the video and the positional audio tracks. In VR, controller models with raycast pointer lines replace the mouse.
